# Reference prompt – female head (Haru)

Purpose: single-image reference for VARCO image-to-3D (Generate3D, tPose=0).
Requirements from docs/handoff (VARCO): one complete subject, nothing cropped,
plain background, no base/platform, no strong cast shadows.
Design goals for this project: clean sculptural surfaces for facial morph
targets (jaw, lips, brows, lids), parted lips so the mesh has a mouth line,
hair as solid volume clearly separated from the face.

## Prompt (used verbatim)

Front view studio photograph of a single contemporary porcelain bust sculpture
of an adult woman, about 30 years old: head, neck and the top of the shoulders,
ending in a clean horizontal cut across the upper chest. No pedestal, no base,
no stand, nothing supporting it.

Pose: perfectly frontal and symmetrical, head level, looking straight ahead
into the camera, shoulders square to the camera.

Material: matte unglazed bisque porcelain in a warm ivory tone, uniform colour
over the whole sculpture, a very faint soft translucency at the thinnest edges
(ears, nostrils). No glaze, no glossy highlights, no cracks, no painted details,
no makeup.

Face: serene, calm, neutral expression with relaxed facial muscles. Eyes open,
sculpted with softly carved irises and pupils as shallow relief (not painted),
clearly defined upper and lower eyelids. Brows sculpted as gentle low relief.
Straight, elegant nose. Full, well-defined lips, gently parted with a narrow
dark gap between upper and lower lip, mouth corners relaxed. Smooth cheeks and
a soft, defined jawline and chin.

Hair: sculpted as one smooth, solid volume with a few broad wave grooves, swept
back from the forehead and temples into a low chignon at the nape, clearly
separated from the face and neck. Ears visible below the hairline. No loose
strands, no flyaways, no fine hair detail.

Lighting: soft, even, diffuse studio light from the front and slightly above,
gentle shading that reveals the forms, no harsh or hard-edged cast shadows,
no rim light.

Background: seamless plain light grey (#d4d4d4), evenly lit, no gradient
banding, no shadow of the bust on the background, nothing else in the frame.

Framing: the entire bust is fully visible and centred with a generous margin on
all sides, nothing cropped. Square 1:1 composition.

Style: museum-quality sculpture photography, photorealistic, sharp focus, high
detail. An original character that does not resemble any real person. No text,
no watermark, no logo, no jewellery, no clothing, no other objects.
